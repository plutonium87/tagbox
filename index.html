Quick guide

Drawing

  • Choose Marker, Spray or Erase, then drag on the canvas. Hold Shift and click to set a straight-line start, move to preview, then click to commit. Press Escape to cancel.
  • Marker tips: Round, Square, Chisel and Split Chisel. Adjust size, smoothing, Paint Load, Run Intensity and Paint Splatter.
  • Spray nozzles: Standard and Calligraphy. Adjust size, smoothing and pressure; Calligraphy also has nozzle rotation.
  • Choose a paint color or Silver, Copper or Gold Chrome. The surface picker offers Solid, Steel Gate, Norrtåg, City Park, Store front and Industrial.

Mouse and keyboard

  • Mouse wheel over the canvas zooms. Middle-button or right-button drag pans; hold Space and left-drag to pan.
  • Undo Stroke or Ctrl+Z (⌘Z on Mac) undoes the last stroke. Clear Canvas or Delete clears the canvas on desktop.
  • Escape exits Fullscreen. Focus a rotation dial and use arrow keys to rotate 1° (Shift+arrow: 5°); Home and End select 0° and 360°.
  • Drag the Navigator map or its viewport rectangle to move around the canvas. Use its toggle to hide or show it.

Touch and sound

  • One-finger touch draws. Two-finger movement on the canvas pinches to zoom and moves the view.
  • Drag the Navigator map or viewport with touch to pan.
  • Sound controls mute all audio or adjust Master, Ambience and Effects separately. Preferences are saved in this browser.
